==================================================
1. CORE OBJECTIVE
==================================================

SENTINEL is a disaster-management web dashboard for authorities.

It must allow authorities to monitor:

- disaster incidents
- affected areas
- estimated population at risk
- shelters
- shelter capacity
- shelter resources
- rescue teams
- rescue operations
- emergency alerts
- environmental conditions
- operational priorities

The website must help an authority quickly answer:

1. What is happening?
2. Where is it happening?
3. Who may be affected?
4. What resources are available?
5. What requires attention right now?
